Write in blocks that become anything

Every line is a block you can reshape — text, headings, to-dos, toggles, callouts, code, images, embeds, and nested sub-pages. Drag to reorder, stack side-by-side, or fold it all away.

  • Rich text, lists, quotes, code & dividers
  • Image covers, carousels, files & video embeds
  • Multi-column layouts and collapsible toggles
  • Nested pages with breadcrumb navigation

Databases, shown your way

Turn any collection into a database and flip between views without copying a thing. The same data as a table, a Kanban board, a calendar, or a timeline — filtered, sorted, and grouped however you like.

  • Table, Board, Calendar & Timeline views
  • Custom properties: status, select, date, text & more
  • Filter, sort, group & colour-code at a glance
  • Every row opens as a full page
